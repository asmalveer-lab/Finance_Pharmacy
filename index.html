Common mistakes to avoid

  • Relying on an old social-media post instead of the official call.
  • Assuming admission automatically includes funding.
  • Using one generic SOP for every programme.
  • Missing document certification, translation or referee requirements.
  • Paying an agent who guarantees admission or a scholarship.
  • Sending money or passport scans to an unverified contact.
  • Ignoring visa financial evidence and application lead times.
Scam warning: no legitimate adviser can guarantee a scholarship, admission or visa decision.
